Research

I am a phenomenologist working at the interface of particle physics, cosmology and machine learning. My work has centred on extensions of the Standard Model with additional gauge symmetries — gauged quintessence, dark photons, and dark gauge-mediated supersymmetry breaking — together with their observational signatures in cosmology, precision electroweak measurements, and laboratory experiments. More recently I have been developing neural-network methods for stitching together analytic solutions to physical problems.
